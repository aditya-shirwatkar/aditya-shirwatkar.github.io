Shield-Loco: Shielding Locomotion Policies with Predictive Safety Filtering

Aditya Shirwatkar, Sebastian Sanokowski, Shishir Kolathaya, Aaron Johnson, Majid Khadiv

arXiv Preprint, 2026  ·  Under Review

arXiv: 2606.07193

Proposes a predictive safety filter that post-hoc filters nominal contact locations fed to RL policies. Uses a sampling-based optimizer with a full-physics model to search for safer contact sequences when collisions are predicted, while a learned value function bootstraps long-horizon returns. Validated on a quadruped in dense, cluttered environments in simulation and the real world.
