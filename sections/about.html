Since elementary school, I've been interested in math and physics. Thanks to my awesome research group [IvLabs], led by Dr. Shital S. Chiddarwar during undergrad at VNIT Nagpur, I had the opportunity to explore different areas in robotics.

After years of adventure, I realized that I enjoyed seeing my robots generate dynamic motions and studying the mathematical beauty of their nonlinear dynamics. This enjoyment has led me to my research interests in Legged Locomotion, Robot Learning, and their intersection.

I'm pursuing my PhD under [Prof. Shishir N. Y.] at the [StochLab], Robert Bosch Centre for Cyber Physical Systems, IISc Bangalore, focusing on developing frameworks for "contact-rich" legged locomotion.

Currently, I'm a Visiting Researcher at the [ATARI Lab], Munich Institute of Robotics and Machine Intelligence (MIRMI), TUM, working with Prof. Majid Khadiv on safe and optimal loco-manipulation problems.

Feel free to contact me for any questions or suggestions. I would be more than happy to interact through a chat or a meet. Besides this, I like to spend my time drawing and looking at trees and clouds :)

Research

Constrained Zeroth-Order Optimization

Interested in how zeroth-order optimization methods can handle constraints that arise in contact-rich locomotion. This direction connects PIP-Loco and Shield-Loco through a shared focus on optimization under contact discontinuities.

Safe Loco-Manipulation

Exploring predictive safety filters for humanoid locomotion and manipulation. Current work at TUM investigates whole-body collision avoidance using full-physics models for humanoid platforms.

Legged Locomotion

Past work on dynamic locomotion for quadrupeds, bipeds, and wheeled-bipeds using MPC, reinforcement learning, and their combination. Validated on Go1, Cassie, Stoch-3, and TronA1-W across challenging terrains.
