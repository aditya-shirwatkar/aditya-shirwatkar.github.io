Since elementary school, I've been interested in math and physics. Thanks to my awesome undergrad research group [IvLabs], led by [Dr. Shital S. Chiddarwar] at VNIT Nagpur, I had the opportunity to explore different areas in robotics.

After years of exploration, I realized that I enjoyed seeing my robots generate dynamic motions and studying the mathematical beauty of their nonlinear dynamics. This enjoyment led me to my research interests in legged locomotion, robot learning, and their intersection.

I'm pursuing my PhD under [Prof. Shishir N. Y.] at the [StochLab], Robert Bosch Centre for Cyber Physical Systems, IISc Bangalore.

Currently, I'm a Visiting Student at the [ATARI Lab], Munich Institute of Robotics and Machine Intelligence (MIRMI), TUM, working with [Prof. Majid Khadiv] and collaborating with [Prof. Aaron M. Johnson].

Feel free to reach out with any questions or suggestions - I'd be more than happy to chat or set up a meeting. Outside of research, I like to spend my time drawing and looking at trees and clouds :)
